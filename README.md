## 🛑 The Problem
Traditional event planning is notoriously stressful, disjointed, and expensive. Planners and hosts juggle multiple spreadsheets, endless email threads with vendors, unorganized moodboards, and chaotic budget trackers—often leading to miscommunication, budget overruns, and last-minute panic.

## 💡 The Solution: EventGenie AI
**EventGenie AI** is the ultimate all-in-one celebration assistant designed to streamline the entire event lifecycle. From dynamic budget calculations and aesthetic AI moodboards to 12-month reverse timelines and automated vendor RFPs, it brings order, elegance, and intelligence to weddings, milestone birthdays, and community gatherings.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **📊 Dynamic Budget Calculator:** Category-wise expense tracking (Venue, Catering, Decor, Photography, Misc) with real-time total calculations and progress bars.
* **🎨 AI Aesthetic Moodboard & Decor Selector:** Interactive gallery featuring curated categories for Venue Atmosphere, Table Settings, Floral & Arches, Lighting & Decor, and Catering Displays[cite: 1].
* **🎨 Color Palette Extractor & Customizer:** Instantly extracts and displays harmonious color palette HEX codes tailored to your chosen event theme[cite: 1].
* **⏳ 12-Month Reverse Timeline:** A structured milestone checklist counting down from 12 months out to the day of the event.
* **✉️ Automated Vendor RFPs:** Generates professional, tailored inquiry templates for caterers, decorators, and venues instantly.
* **🔐 Seamless Authentication:** Quick onboarding flow with multi-method login options and event type selection (Wedding, Birthday, Community Event, Corporate Gala).

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack Used

* **Frontend:** React (with Vite) for blazing-fast performance and modular component architecture.
* **Styling:** Tailwind CSS for a modern, responsive, luxury dark-themed UI.
* **Icons & UI Elements:** Lucide React icons for clean, modern iconography.
* **State Management:** React Hooks (`useState`, `useEffect`) for seamless interactivity across modules.
